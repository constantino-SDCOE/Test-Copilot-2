Practice awareness.

The Phishing Derby is a cybersecurity awareness initiative designed to help participants identify common phishing techniques and make thoughtful choices online.

Use this page to follow the project overview, see clearly labeled example metrics, and revisit a few practical ways to check a message before acting.

Phishing tips

A quick checklist for your inbox.

  • 01Check the sender's address, not just the display name.
  • 02Pause before opening unexpected links or attachments.
  • 03Be cautious when a message creates urgency or asks for sensitive information.
  • 04Report suspicious messages using your organization's approved process.
